OUR STORY

Crafting modern heirlooms in 18K solid gold and certified diamonds — designed to be worn, cherished, and passed down through generations.

THE FOUNDATION

TIMELESS DESIGN, REFINED WITH PURPOSE

ROYRA JEWELS was founded on a singular conviction: fine jewellery shouldn't be locked away in vaults for rare occasions. It should accompany you through your everyday triumphs, quiet reflections, and milestone memories.

Rooted in India's rich artisanal heritage and influenced by contemporary European minimalism, our atelier blends age-old hand-setting techniques with state-of-the-art metallurgical refinement.

Every curve, prong, and setting is engineered for tactile comfort, lasting structural integrity, and maximum light refraction.

UNCOMPROMISING STANDARDS

THE ROYRA PILLARS

What sets every piece of Royra fine jewellery apart.

18K BIS 916 Hallmarked

We work exclusively in solid 18K yellow, rose, and white gold, tested and hallmarked under the strict government BIS framework.

Certified Stones

Every diamond and gemstone is rigorously hand-inspected for optical brilliance, color uniformity, and conflict-free origin.

Lifetime Service

We stand behind our creations with complimentary lifetime ultrasonic cleaning, prong inspections, and polishing support.

CONSCIOUS LUXURY

ETHICAL SOURCING & CIRCULAR GOLD

At Royra Jewels, luxury and ecological responsibility exist in complete harmony. Over 85% of the gold used across our atelier is recycled and re-refined, radically reducing our environmental footprint.

All stones conform with the Kimberley Process and responsible mineral governance. Furthermore, our signature presentation cases are crafted from FSC-certified sustainable timber and biodegradable velvet linings.
